## Original assets

Inventory/buff icons generated with OpenAI's built-in image generation tool, then
resized to transparent 128 x 128 PNG. Original generated files are preserved in
`assets-source/generated-icons/`. Prompts are recorded in `assets-source/ICON-PROMPTS.txt`.
The earlier synthesized test loops were generated by build/Generate-TestAudio.ps1.
They are not included in the current eleven-song package.
